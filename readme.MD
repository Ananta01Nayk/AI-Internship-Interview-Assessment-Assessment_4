# Multi-Language Patient Communication System

## Problem Overview
Apollo Clinic in Chennai has a multilingual patient population that speaks Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Hindi, and English. But its English-only SMS system leads to low confirmation rates (35%) and is challenging for non-English speakers and old patients.

## Proposed Solution
An AI-driven multi-channel communication system that:
- Addresses patients by their name with personalized messages based on saved language preferences.
- Applies SMS, WhatsApp, and IVR (Voice AI) for improved accessibility.
- Applies NLP-based translation to communicate in regional languages.
- Applies AI-driven voice reminders to elderly patients.

## Evaluation Tasks
1. Multi-Channel Strategy: Create a channel selection algorithm based on patient demographics.
2. AI-Powered Communication: Apply NLP-based translation & voice AI for effective communication.
3. Measuring Effectiveness: Utilize tracking of confirmation rate, A/B testing, and patient feedback survey.
